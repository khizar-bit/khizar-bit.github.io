Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026. This policy covers Wrong Angle Studios YouTube Publisher, a personal desktop tool operated by Khizar, and this app-information website.

Google and YouTube data

After the operator authorizes Google OAuth, the application accesses the authorized channel’s ID, name, handle, upload playlist, video metadata, thumbnails, processing status, visibility, audience designation and playlists. It sends selected video files, thumbnails and publishing metadata to YouTube to perform the operator’s publishing instructions.

The application requests the YouTube youtube.force-ssl permission. Google describes this permission as including viewing, editing and permanently deleting YouTube videos, ratings, comments and captions. The application implements publishing operations; it does not implement deletion or comment/rating collection.

Use, storage and sharing

Data is used to upload and manage the operator’s channel content, verify processing, prevent duplicate uploads, and maintain publication records. OAuth client credentials, access/refresh tokens and resumable-upload session URLs are stored in Windows Credential Manager. Local files and SQLite records retain file hashes, paths, publishing metadata, parent-video relationships, IDs, status, timestamps and operation evidence.

Selected content and metadata are transmitted to Google/YouTube. The application does not sell Google user data, share it with advertising services, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Public publication occurs only when explicitly enabled by the operator. This information site does not host uploaded videos, OAuth tokens or the local publishing database. Ordinary hosting/network requests are processed by GitHub Pages under its own privacy practices. The site adds no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, forms or Google login.

Retention and removal

Local credentials and publication records remain until the operator removes them. Publication records are retained to prevent duplicates. Removing local records does not remove videos from YouTube. The operator can stop the worker, remove the app’s Windows Credential Manager entries and local publication data, and manage or delete uploaded content directly in YouTube.

Google access can be revoked at Google Account permissions. YouTube content remains governed by the operator’s channel settings. Contact dumdumsoup26@gmail.com for questions or data-removal requests related to this tool.
